**Slide line that holds up:** *"Chile ships USD 2.8 bn of cherries a season, ~87% to China. Chilean SMEs wait 44 days on average to get paid, and 63% of what they invoice to large companies is paid after the 30-day legal limit. When cash runs short, banks charge them 20–33% a year."* Footnote: *FreshFruitPortal (Frutas de Chile data) and FreshPlaza; Bolsa de Productos / ASECH, Ranking de Pagadores (Q3 2024; 44 days: Jul 2025–Jun 2026, invoices to large domestic payers); CMF certificate 09/2026 (20.06% for 90 days or more, 33.34% under 90 days).* Say "to large companies" and "after 30 days": the ranking only covers invoices to large payers, and "late" means past the legal term.
