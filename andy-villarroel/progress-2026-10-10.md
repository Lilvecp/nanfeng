## The idea in one line

The AI proposes, it never decides about money. A deterministic policy engine decides the level, the owner approves anything important with a one-time SMS code, and every step is hash-chained and anchored on chain.

## What was built

### 1. Policy engine — r4-02 (`backend/app/policy.py`)

Replaces Camilo's placeholder. Every money request gets a level:

| Level | Meaning | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| L1 | Runs alone, owner notified | Payment ≤ CLP 1M to a known payee |
| L2 | Runs alone within limits | Payment ≤ CLP 25M; agent spend ≤ 40M / 24 h, ≤ 80M / 30 days |
| L3 | Owner + SMS code | Factoring, loans, forwards, new payee, account changes, anything over a limit |
| Blocked | Not even the owner from the app | > CLP 400M, sanctions match, kill switch on |

It also catches split payments (structuring), too many actions too fast (velocity), and operations packed into one request. Limits match the Settings screen and are served at `GET /policy`. Decision and execution run under one lock (race condition verified by a test).

### 2. Step-up OTP — r4-05 (`backend/app/otp.py`)

- `NANFENG_OTP_MODE=sms`: random code per approval, sent by Zavu (`POST https://api.zavu.dev/v1/messages`).
- Only an HMAC of `approval | amount | code` is stored: single use, 5 minutes, bound to that approval and amount, never in logs or SSE.
- The agent token cannot approve, reject or turn the kill switch off. Voice channels never approve L3.
- Sandbox mode (shared demo code) still works for the demo.

### 3. BEC anti-fraud and document guard — r4-03 / r4-04

- `POST /security/scan` (`guard.py`): instructions aimed at the AI in ES / EN / 中文, hidden PDF text, invisible Unicode, base64-encoded instructions, exfiltration requests, urgency.
- `POST /security/check-payee` (`bec.py`): changed bank account, look-alike domain, unknown issuer → block, return the call-back number on file, SMS alert to the owner.
- Payee account changes are an L3 operation that needs a call-back record; payments to a payee whose account changed in the last 72 h go to the owner.

### 4. Privacy — r4-07 (`privacy.py`, `sanctions.py`)

- `POST /privacy/mask`: RUT, PRC ID, phones, e-mails, bank accounts → placeholders before any LLM call.
- Sanctions screening (synthetic watchlist) on every counterparty; a hit blocks.
- The audit log shows only the last four digits of accounts.

### 5. Other fixes

- The agent can no longer write audit entries as `owner` (actor comes from the token).
- The agent can turn the kill switch on, never off.
